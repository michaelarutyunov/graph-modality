Is the graph signal relational — or just distributional?

The founding thesis was that concept-graph structure — how ideas wire together — carries signal that flat text cannot. After a string of nulls (ADR-0006), this is the final, pre-scoped retest on the v4 corpus: give the relational hypothesis its strongest fair shot, then accept the answer. Three tests, one verdict.

B1 Endpoint-aware conflict edges RELATIONAL · FAIL

Ambivalence is holding conflicting stances, so the CONFLICTS_WITH edge should be where topology pays off. The data first surprised us: 425/425 conflict edges connect Construct→Construct, never Stance→Stance, and they are sparse — 880 of 1,250 graphs have none. So the decisive feature had to be genuinely relational: a conflict edge joining two constructs the respondent feels oppositely about (valence reached via Stance –EXPRESSED_VIA→ Construct).

Why this is a false positive, not a finding

B1's first pass passed against a thin 9-dim baseline (+0.042) — and would have "confirmed" the relational hypothesis. The decomposition reveals the entire gain is the raw conflict count, which is near-circular: a count of conflict edges predicting ambivalence is almost measuring the label twice. Strip that, and the genuinely relational opposite-valence feature is sub-threshold — and worse than the count. Conflict structure adds nothing once circularity is removed.

C RGCN — the strongest fair edge encoder RELATIONAL · FAIL

Both cheap tests came back null, so the gate opened to the confirmatory shot: a per-relation graph network (RGCNConv, distinct weights per relation type) — strictly more edge-expressive than the additive-edge GINEConv. Added as a 4th rung to the structure-only edge ladder so all encoders train under identical capacity, objective, and splits. Node features are structure-only (type + degree) — no label semantics — to isolate the edge axis.

∎ Triangulated verdict ADR-0007 · ACCEPTED
What the graph modality IS

A structured bag of typed, valenced concepts. Its predictive signal is distributional node-attribute — which concepts appear and how the respondent feels about them — and it is conceptual, not lexical (B2). On a lexically-non-obvious, independently-labelled target, these attributes carry signal flat text cannot recover.

What it is NOT

Relational / topological. How concepts wire together adds nothing: endpoint-aware conflict structure fails net of circularity (B1), and a per-relation GNN with extra capacity cannot beat untyped wiring (RGCN). The relational thread is closed — falsified on evidence, not just argument.

Three independent angles, one direction

B1 (endpoint-aware features) · B2 (lexical control) · RGCN (per-relation encoder) all confirm ADR-0006. The most important moment was catching B1's false PASS: a naive pipeline would have shipped "+0.042, p<0.05, relational confirmed." The discipline of testing against the strongest baseline and decomposing for circularity is what separated a finding from a tautology. A null this thoroughly triangulated is a strong, defensible result — not a disappointment.
